# Reflection

Among the three cloud platforms, **Amazon Web Services (AWS)** impressed me the most because of its wide range of services and global infrastructure. I learned that AWS can support different types of organizations, from small startups to large international businesses. Its scalability and variety of services make it a flexible platform for different cloud computing needs.

AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP) have many similarities. All three provide essential cloud services such as virtual machines, storage, databases, networking, security, and application development tools. They also provide global infrastructure, management consoles, scalable resources, and services that allow businesses to move their systems to the cloud. Although the platforms offer similar services, their features and strengths can make one platform more suitable for a specific organization.

Business requirements greatly influence cloud platform selection. Factors such as budget, existing technology, performance requirements, scalability, security, and business location should be considered. For example, Azure is a good choice for a university that already uses Microsoft products, while GCP can be suitable for companies focused on artificial intelligence and machine learning. This showed me that there is no single cloud platform that is best for every organization.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me how important it is to understand the client's needs before recommending a technology. I learned to compare cloud platforms based on practical requirements rather than simply choosing the platform I prefer. I also learned how different cloud services can work together to solve specific business problems.

After completing this mission, my **GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio** has improved because it now contains organized research, screenshots, recommendations, and reflections about AWS, Azure, and GCP. It demonstrates not only my understanding of cloud platforms but also my ability to analyze real-world scenarios and make technology recommendations.
